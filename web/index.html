🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy Guarantee

Oryn connects directly to your Google Gmail via OAuth 2.0 PKCE. All email processing, entity extraction, and classification happen exclusively inside your device's memory using local MMKV binary storage. No personal data, email content, or login credentials are ever sent to an external server.
